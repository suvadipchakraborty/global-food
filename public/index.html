Food is geography you can taste.

Culinary Geography Explorer pairs two datasets that rarely sit at the same table: hard demographic facts about a place, and the food its people actually cook. The idea is simple — a population figure means more once you've smelled the spices behind it.

Why demographics + dinner

A country is usually explained to you in one of two ways: as a set of statistics, or as a cuisine you order at a restaurant. Rarely both at once. We think the two illuminate each other — climate and history shape what grows and what's eaten, and what's eaten is one of the most honest records of a culture's past.

How it works

Every waypoint on the atlas is a real place. Tapping it asks two independent, open datasets for the truth: population, region and languages from a country data registry, and a genuine, highly-rated recipe tied to that region's cuisine. Nothing is scripted — if a source is briefly unavailable, we say so rather than make something up.
